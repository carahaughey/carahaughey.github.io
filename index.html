About

Health, policy, and the social safety net.

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at the University of Richmond. I earned my Ph.D. in Economics at Michigan State University in 2026.

My primary fields are health economics and public finance, with a particular emphasis on social determinants of health. I also draw on environmental and urban economics. My current research examines the demand for and provision of charitable social services, including food pantries.
